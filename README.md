# PROJECT-NULL (v0.1.0)

**PROJECT-NULL** is a browser-based incremental (idle) management game deeply inspired by the atmosphere and core loop of ***Lobotomy Corporation***.

> **Current Version:** `v0.1.0`
> *Note: This is an early prototype (Alpha). Gameplay mechanics, balancing, and UI are subject to change.*

##  Overview
In this project, you take on the role of a containment terminal operator. You are tasked with managing a facility and observing an anomalous entity known only as the **"Tree of Nihility."** 

By assigning employees to interact with the tree, you extract "Essence"—the primary resource required to unlock the tree's hidden potential. As you upgrade the tree's core nodes, you will uncover new gameplay mechanics, alter how your employees interact with the anomaly, and delve deeper into the unknown.

##  Key Features
- **Atmospheric UI**: A dark, terminal-inspired interface featuring scanlines and eerie visual feedback that pays homage to SCP and Lobotomy Corporation.
- **Incremental Progression**: Watch your Essence grow, manage your sanity, and make strategic choices between high-yield extraction and fast but risky observation cycles.
- **Tree Node Upgrades**: Click on the nodes of the Tree of Nihility to purchase upgrades, complete with particle effects and visual tree growth.
- **Interactive Canvas**: Pan and zoom around the containment unit (Right-click + Drag to pan, Scroll to zoom) to inspect the tree.
- **Multi-language Support**: Fully localized in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- **Local Save System**: Progress is saved automatically to your browser. You can also export/import your save data as files or text strings.

## 🛠️ How to Play
Since this is a pure HTML/CSS/JS project, you can simply:
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Or, play it online via **GitHub Pages** (See deployment guide below).
